# Money-Page & SEO Expansion Plan — drnitinsunkuortho.com

_Researched: July 18, 2026. Scope: SERP sampling for Attibele-catchment queries + competitor site teardowns + current site inventory (sitemap, services, treatments, 70+ blog posts)._

## The honest framing first

For "orthopedic doctor Attibele"-class queries, page one is a map pack followed by directories (Practo, Lybrate, Credihealth, JustDial, HexaHealth, Apollo 24|7). New pages on your own site compete for the 2–4 organic slots that remain. The July 4 off-site items (GBP verification, review drip, citations) are still the single biggest ranking lever and remain pending. Build these pages — they are the right move and several gaps are genuinely undefended — but ship them alongside the GBP/citation work, not instead of it.

The competitive reality that makes page expansion worth it anyway: the two clinics beating you organically are winning **on page inventory, not authority**. Vijaya Ortho (Dr. Prashanth B, physically in Attibele) holds the top organic slot with 11 condition/service pages. City Ortho Clinic (Electronic City) ranks across the whole southeast corridor with **eight geo landing pages** — Attibele, Anekal, Chandapura, Hosur Road, Jigani, Bannerghatta Road, Sarjapur, HSR — many of them thin doorway-style pages. Your site has stronger technical SEO and deeper clinical content than either; it simply covers fewer of the queries people actually type.

## Tier 1 — build these first (highest intent × weakest competition)

### 1. `/fracture-treatment-attibele` — Fracture, Trauma & Accident Care

The biggest gap on the site relative to what an Attibele **hospital** actually sells. Attibele sits on an industrial belt (Attibele Industrial Area, Bommasandra–Jigani corridor, Hosur Road traffic), and fracture/accident queries are urgent, high-converting, and map-pack-plus-one-organic-click searches. Vijaya Ortho has a "Trauma & Fractures" page; you have a generic `/services/bone-fracture` service page with no local intent, no "same-day", no emergency framing. Target: "fracture treatment near me", "fracture clinic Attibele", "accident orthopedic care Hosur Road", "hand/wrist/ankle fracture doctor Bangalore". Content: casting vs surgery decision, same-day X-ray availability at the hospital, industrial-injury and two-wheeler-accident framing, ESI/insurance note, emergency contact block. This page also does double duty as the hospital-branding page the project exists for.

### 2. `/orthopedic-doctor-electronic-city`

Already flagged P2 in the July 4 audit; the research confirms it. Electronic City has multiples of Attibele's search volume, and the current #1 organic result (City Ortho Clinic, in Hulimangala) plus a blog-post-as-landing-page from Minchu Health Care are beatable on content quality. Your Attibele clinic is a 15–20 minute drive. Distinct angle: IT-corridor audience — desk-related back/neck pain, weekend-sports injuries, corporate insurance/cashless, evening OPD timings, commute directions from Phase 1/2.

### 3. `/orthopedic-doctor-chandapura` (covering Anekal in-page)

Chandapura–Anekal Road is a dense, fast-growing corridor with essentially **zero strong local organic result** — the SERP is all directories plus City Ortho's thin doorway page. Lowest difficulty of all the geo targets, and geographically honest (Chandapura is ~10 min from Attibele). Fold Anekal into the same page with an H2 rather than building a fourth thin geo page; split it out later only if impressions justify it.

### 4. `/knee-replacement-cost-bangalore` — upgrade from blog post to money page

Cost queries are their own SERP category dominated by aggregators (Practo, HexaHealth, Pristyn, Medfin) — but individual surgeon sites do rank on page one for them. You already have the content as a blog post; rebuild it as a conversion page: cost range table (implant type × room category), what insurance covers, cashless process at the hospital, robotic/conventional comparison, EMI, "get an exact quote" lead form. 301 the blog post into it. Repeat later for ACL surgery cost and PRP/GFC cost if this one performs.

## Tier 3 — differentiators competitors haven't defended

- **`/orthopedic-doctor-near-hosur`** — Attibele is at the Karnataka–TN border; Hosur's industrial workforce crosses it daily. Competition (Kauvery, Malar) sits on the Hosur side and nobody on the Bangalore side targets "orthopedic doctor near Hosur". Frame honestly as "10 minutes from Hosur, on the Bangalore side" to avoid fake-location risk.
- **`/insurance-cashless-orthopedic-treatment`** — ESI, corporate TPA cashless, Ayushman Bharat/Arogya applicability at the hospital. Almost no local competitor has this page; it is a conversion multiplier for every other page (link it site-wide) and a natural fit for an industrial-belt patient base. Requires accurate input from the hospital on what is actually empaneled — do not publish guesses.
- **`/pediatric-orthopedic-bangalore`** — only if Dr. Nitin genuinely treats children's fractures/deformities. Vijaya lists it; family catchment in Attibele/Sarjapura is growing. Skip if it's not a real service line.
- **Kannada-language Attibele page** (`/kn/` variant of the Attibele page) — optional; local-language searches exist for clinic queries and zero competitors serve them. Cheap test, but only after Tiers 1–2.

## What NOT to build

No mass geo-page rollout (Jigani, Bommasandra, Bannerghatta, Whitefield…) copying City Ortho — that's doorway-page territory, and post-helpful-content Google is actively demoting it; their pages rank only because no one competes. Three to four geo pages with real, distinct content is the ceiling before it becomes a liability. No "Sarjapur Road" page for now — that SERP belongs to Manipal, Sparsh, and Sakra hospital pages; you'd be outgunned on authority (revisit if GSC shows impressions). No robotic-surgery page unless the hospital actually has the system. No aggregateRating schema, per the July 4 decision.

## Sequencing and measurement

Ship order: fracture/trauma page → Electronic City → knee-replacement-cost → Chandapura → then one Tier 2 page per 2–3 weeks (steady publishing beats a bulk drop). Every new page: absolute title (the title-template bug pattern), LandingFaq + schema graph, entry in `app/sitemap.ts` with a bumped `SITE_CONTENT_UPDATED`, internal links added to `lib/internal-links.ts` from the matching blog posts, and GSC re-index request on deploy. Track per-page impressions/position in GSC and form/WhatsApp/call events in GA4 (already instrumented). Expect geo pages to show impressions in 2–4 weeks and meaningful positions in 8–12; cost pages take longer against aggregators.
